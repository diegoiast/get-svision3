SVision3 is an object oriented cross platform retained toolkit in C++.

Its is designed with C++20 in mind, and tries to re-use platform components and 
3rd parties.

It is licensed under the MIT license - to be usable for all, with as minimal 
restrictions as possible.

Some of the features:
1. Multi platform (Windows, macOS, Linux).
1. Mutli drawing engine (GDI+, Cocoa, Cairo - or OpenGL).
1. On Linux supports Wayland and X11.
1. Native performace (C++20).
1. Many widgets available
1. Markdown support (Using [LiteHTML](https://github.com/litehtml/litehtml))
1. Serialization of UI to JSON (and de-serialization).
